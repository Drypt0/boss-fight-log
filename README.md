# Boss Fight Log

A RuneLite side panel that keeps a per-kill record of your boss fights.

Where the Loot Tracker shows you *what you got*, Boss Fight Log shows you *how you did*: kill time,
DPS, damage taken and accuracy for every boss kill, plus running totals and bests across the session.

## Scope

This plugin is **panel-only and post-kill**. It draws nothing in the game world, has no overlay, and
surfaces no information while a fight is in progress — a kill card appears only once the fight has
already resolved. It is a record of what happened, not an aid to what is happening.
